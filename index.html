Welcome to the TCG Tattoo Card Game International Project

You're here because you've been invited to take part in the first-ever worldwide tattoo artist collaboration — a chance to have your artwork immortalized in a spectacular limited-edition trading card collection.

Perks: every card featuring your work doubles as free advertising for you. Select ultra-rare cards will also carry real-world value — each one acts as a gift card redeemable directly with the artist whose design appears on it. Royalties are paid out on redemption, and can be exchanged for tattooing time with that artist.

Submission guidelines: submissions must be original artwork — drawings, paintings, or digital designs. Photographs of tattoos will not be accepted.

Please include your social media links, studio name or website, and anything else you'd like featured — a short bio or a meaningful quote works well. This information, along with your location, will appear on the back of your card.
